About Versa Femme

Unbeatable quality.
Affordable, always.

Versa Femme (also written VersaFemme) is a wholesale clothing supplier based in Gandhi Nagar, Delhi NCR. We believe great clothing should not carry a luxury markup. By keeping our designs simple and our supply chain short, we pass the savings on to our wholesale partners.

I

Quality at Affordable Prices

Refined pieces that feel expensive and are priced fairly for resellers, so quality is never a compromise.

II

Minimalist, Premium Materials

Plain, timeless designs crafted from premium fabrics. Nothing loud, everything considered.

III

Direct From Factory

We source straight from our factory in Gandhi Nagar, Delhi NCR and supply wholesalers with no middlemen, keeping prices honest and quality consistent.
