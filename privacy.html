Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Versa Femme ("we", "us", "our") is a wholesale clothing supplier operating from Gandhi Nagar, Delhi NCR, India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you visit versafemme.vercel.app or place a wholesale order with us.

Information We Collect

When you place an order through our website, we collect only the details you provide directly:

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.
